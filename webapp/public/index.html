        Placing the boards

        1. Receiver in a corner, 0.8 to 1.0 m off the floor, antenna end facing the room. Its USB cable goes to this computer.
        2. Transmitter on the back of the chair, centred, 25 to 35 cm above the seat. Fix it firmly: a node that wobbles makes its own “breathing”.
        3. Chair 2 to 4 m from the receiver, facing it, so the straight line from the transmitter to the receiver passes through the chest.
        4. Sit still and breathe normally for 30 seconds. While you adjust a board, watch Pattern strength: it rises when the placement works.
